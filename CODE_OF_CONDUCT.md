# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want QueryDoctor to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to learn, contribute or
ask questions, regardless of experience level, background, identity or the country they write
code from.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Credit other people's ideas and effort.
- Keep discussions on topic and respect other people's time.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Personal attacks, doxxing or sharing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention directed at a person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the repository's
**Security** tab or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately and in good
faith.

This code of conduct applies in the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests and any
space where someone represents the project.
